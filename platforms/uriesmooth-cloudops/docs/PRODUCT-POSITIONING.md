# Uriesmooth CloudOps — Product Positioning

## Official identity

**Product:** Uriesmooth CloudOps  
**Owner:** Uriesmooth Group  
**Technology division:** UriesmoothTech Ltd  
**Category:** Shared cloud operations, deployment governance and infrastructure automation platform.

## Positioning statement

For Uriesmooth Group engineering, platform and operations teams that need to deliver and operate digital products across multiple environments, **Uriesmooth CloudOps** is the shared cloud operations platform that brings infrastructure visibility, deployment coordination, policy governance, cost awareness and operational accountability into one reusable control layer.

Unlike provider-specific scripts or product-specific deployment pipelines, CloudOps is designed around provider-neutral contracts, least-privilege identities, auditable operations and explicit approval gates.

## Tagline

**One governed infrastructure foundation. Multiple products. Global operational readiness.**

## Short description

Uriesmooth CloudOps helps Uriesmooth Group teams understand, deploy and govern cloud infrastructure through secure inventory, controlled release workflows, cost visibility and auditable automation.

## Intended users

- Platform and infrastructure engineers.
- DevOps and release engineers.
- Security and governance teams.
- Product engineering teams using shared infrastructure.
- Authorized operations and finance stakeholders.

## Core value pillars

1. **Visibility before action:** read-only inventory, resource ownership and operational state.
2. **Safer delivery:** repeatable build and deployment workflows with environment protections.
3. **Governed automation:** policy checks, explicit approvals and recoverable operations.
4. **Cost accountability:** usage, quota and budget visibility without destructive automatic cleanup.
5. **Reusable foundations:** common capabilities for Group products without merging their data or runtime boundaries.

## Product boundaries

CloudOps is not:
- an OCI-only product;
- a general-purpose application hosting provider;
- a replacement for product-specific backends;
- a source of truth for product/customer business data;
- an autonomous system authorized to perform destructive operations;
- a promise of free cloud infrastructure or regulatory certification.

OCI is the initial optional provider adapter. Additional providers may be added behind stable interfaces when justified.

## First release promise

The first release is a read-only Inventory MVP. It will show what approved infrastructure exists and provide sanitized, auditable reports. It will not create, change or delete infrastructure. Deployment automation and controlled Apply operations are later milestones subject to independent gates.
