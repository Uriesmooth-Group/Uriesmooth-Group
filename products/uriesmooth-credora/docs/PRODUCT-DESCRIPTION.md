# Uriesmooth Credora — Official Product Description

**Public identity:** Credora by Uriesmooth  
**Tagline:** Financial flexibility. Engineered for progress.  
**Supporting line:** Buy today. Pay responsibly. Build tomorrow.  
**Product owner:** Uriesmooth Group  
**Technology company:** UriesmoothTech Ltd  
**Category:** Financial technology (FinTech)  
**Initial market configuration:** Nigeria-first  
**Expansion direction:** International

## Short description

Uriesmooth Credora is a financial technology platform in development, designed to connect flexible purchasing, responsible credit, installment repayment and financial management. Its planned capabilities include Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL), loan servicing workflows, payment and wallet orchestration, VTU and bill payments, rewards, debt repayment coordination, and credit education and monitoring.

## Official customer-facing description

Get eligible products and services today and spread the cost over manageable payments. **Credora PayLater**, Uriesmooth Credora's Buy Now, Pay Later service, is designed to make responsible shopping more flexible by allowing eligible customers to pay for an approved purchase through a clear installment schedule instead of paying the full amount upfront.

Before accepting an offer, customers should be able to review the purchase amount, total amount repayable, applicable fees or financing charges, installment amounts, due dates, missed-payment terms and agreement conditions. Approval and available terms depend on eligibility, affordability assessment, participating providers and applicable law.

Credora is being designed to make repayment easier to understand and manage. Customers should have access to their agreement details, upcoming installments, payment history, outstanding obligations, receipts, and support or dispute channels.

Credora's broader product direction extends beyond BNPL to include loan management, payment orchestration, VTU and bill payments, rewards, debt repayment coordination, credit education, authorized credit-data monitoring and customer trust controls.

**Credora's commitment is transparency, responsible financial access, secure transactions and clear customer communication.** It is not a promise of guaranteed credit approval, improved credit scores or access to every financial product.

## Product portfolio

### 1. Credora PayLater — Buy Now, Pay Later

Enable eligible customers to finance approved purchases from participating merchants through disclosed installment plans.

Planned capabilities:
- Merchant and product eligibility.
- Customer identity and affordability checks.
- Configurable credit limits and installment structures.
- Clear total-cost and repayment disclosures.
- Customer agreement acceptance and consent evidence.
- Purchase authorization and merchant settlement coordination.
- Installment scheduling, reminders and payment tracking.
- Failed-payment handling, reconciliation, disputes and support.

### 2. Credora Loans

Support loan application and servicing workflows through appropriately authorized lenders or financing partners.

Planned capabilities:
- Applications and eligibility workflow.
- Credit policy and affordability decisioning.
- Offer and agreement presentation.
- Disbursement coordination.
- Repayment schedules and servicing.
- Delinquency workflows, customer support and reconciliation.

### 3. Credora Wallet and Payments

Provide account and payment experiences only under a legally reviewed operating model and appropriate licensed or authorized provider arrangements.

Planned capabilities:
- Account and transaction views.
- Payment initiation and status tracking.
- Funding and payout orchestration through approved providers.
- Reconciliation, refunds and dispute workflows.
- Transaction notifications and receipts.

### 4. Credora VTU

Connect customers to supported digital services through contracted providers, potentially including airtime, mobile data, electricity and cable television. Availability, provider coverage, pricing and fulfilment depend on verified integrations and contracts.

### 5. Credora Rewards

Provide configurable customer loyalty, cashback and referral programs with transparent rules, funding controls, fraud prevention and auditable reward issuance.

### 6. Credora DebtCare

Help customers organize obligations and manage repayment planning, settlement coordination and dispute records. Clearly distinguish software tools from services delivered through authorized partners or regulated debt-related activities.

### 7. Credora CreditScore

Provide credit education and, where authorized, access to credit information, monitoring and correction workflows through approved data partners. Do not promise score improvements or represent an internal estimate as an official bureau score.

### 8. Credora Trust

Provide shared controls that protect the platform and its customers:
- Identity and access management.
- Consent and authorization records.
- Fraud prevention and risk signals.
- Audit trails and transaction monitoring.
- Privacy and data lifecycle controls.
- Customer complaints, disputes and escalation.

## Customer promise

Credora is designed around five commitments:

1. **Clarity:** explain the amount, cost, repayment dates and material terms before acceptance.
2. **Responsibility:** use documented eligibility and affordability controls.
3. **Control:** provide accessible agreement, transaction and repayment information.
4. **Security:** protect customer information and financial operations.
5. **Accountability:** maintain auditable records and meaningful support and dispute processes.

## Product positioning

Credora is not simply a checkout installment feature. Its long-term direction is an integrated financial technology platform connecting eligible customers, participating merchants and authorized financial-service providers through responsible credit, payment orchestration and repayment management.

**Brand statement:** Credora by Uriesmooth makes financial flexibility more transparent, manageable and responsible.

## Availability and regulatory notice

This document describes product direction and intended capabilities. It does not establish that a feature is currently available, that Uriesmooth is a lender or payment institution, that a provider integration is active, or that any license or regulatory approval has been obtained.

Before offering a financial service in any country, Uriesmooth must verify the operating model, applicable licensing and authorization requirements, consumer-protection obligations, data-protection rules, provider contracts, disclosures, complaint handling and operational controls for that market.
